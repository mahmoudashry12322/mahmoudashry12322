## 🚀 Featured Enterprise Projects & Architectural Showcases

> **Note on Proprietary Code:** To safeguard client confidentiality, trade secrets, and business intellectual property, client-commissioned ERP source code is maintained in secure private repositories. Live production deployments and architectural breakdowns are highlighted below:

### 1. 🏭 [Al-Attar Distribution & Manufacturing ERP](https://alattar.online)
*Enterprise ERP platform managing manufacturing cycles, raw material economics, wholesale sales distribution, and real-time field operations.*
- **Production URL:** [https://alattar.online](https://alattar.online)
- **Tech Stack:** React 19, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, PM2, Nginx.
- **Architectural Highlights:**
  - **Zero Double-Counting Financial Ledger:** Unified multi-category expense engine connected directly with worker payrolls and sales representatives' advances via bi-directional database transactions.
  - **Batch Manufacturing Engine:** Bill of Materials (BOM) management calculating unit economics, automatic raw materials depletion (fragrance oils, alcohol solvents, glassware), and packaging yields.
  - **Field Operations & GPS Tracking:** Geo-fenced client visit logging, route optimization, customer balances, and real-time order dispatching for field representatives.
  - **Dynamic P&L and Balance Sheet Reporting:** Real-time financial analytics, daily cashier closures, and multi-branch cash-flow monitoring.

### 2. 📊 Mohasby (محاسبي) – Egyptian Unified Accounting System ERP
*Complete Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform implementing the Egyptian Unified Chart of Accounts (دليل الحسابات المصري الموحد).*
- **Tech Stack:** Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS.
- **Architectural Highlights:**
  - Strict compliance with Egyptian unified accounting regulations, automated double-entry journal entries (قيود اليومية), and multi-level general ledgers (دفتر الأستاذ).
  - Dynamic trial balance (ميزان المراجعة) generation with real-time balance sheet (قائمة المركز المالي) and income statements (قائمة الدخل).
  - Multi-branch fiscal year closing, cost center allocation, and automated debit/credit balancing validation.
